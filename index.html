Questions

Good to know before you join.

Can't find your answer? Our team replies in the app any time, day or night.

How fast does money arrive in Nigeria?

Most transfers to Nigerian banks and mobile wallets land in under a minute. A small number are checked by our team first, which can take up to two hours.

What do I need to open an account?

A passport or national ID, proof of where you live now, and a phone that can take a selfie. Most people are done in about four minutes.

Is my money protected?

In this concept, balances would be held with a regulated partner bank and protected up to £85,000. Kova itself is a fictional brand.

Can I get paid into my pound wallet?

Yes. Your pound wallet has its own sort code and account number, so you can give it to your employer like any UK account.

Which countries can I send to?

Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, India, the Philippines and 37 more. The app shows the speed and fee for each before you send.
